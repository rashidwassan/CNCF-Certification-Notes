### Essential commands to be more productive and save time while usig VIM

- `I` capital I instead of small i moves cursor to the start of the selected line.
- `W` can be pressed in command mode to traverse between or jump to next word.
- `E` moves cursor to end of the word and then next word so on.
- `F` followed by any charcter finds the next occurence of that character in that line.
- `4FT` will find the 4th occurence of T in the line.

### Traversal:
- `gg` will take you to the top line or the first line.
- `Shift+g` will take cursor to the beginning of the last line.
- `Shift+m` will take you to the middle of the visible screen content.
- `zz` will bring the highlighted line in the middle of the current view.
- `p` is used to paste.
- `u` to undo last done change.
